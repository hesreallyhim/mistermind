# Fair Play

This room system is meant for personal entertainment, community participation, and learning through play. Moderation is intentionally lenient at the outset: reminders and monitoring stay in place, and automatic bans are not part of the default policy right now. This may change according to the circumstances.

Always read `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` before participating.

1. Active games are owner-driven. Watching someone else's room is fine, but non-owner gameplay comments are discouraged and may be logged.

2. Don't spam your own room. Wait for the bot to answer before sending another command, and use the documented guess syntax.

3. You may only play one game at a time. If you find that to be too easy, go ahead and try "Perfectionist Mode" - it's a nice challenge.

4. Service protection. During heavy load, new room creation may be paused so active games can finish cleanly. In severe abuse or overload conditions, room interactions may be temporarily restricted.

5. Automation. At the initial phase, fully automated play is forbidden. You may ask an assistant for strategy help, but you may not let a bot play on your behalf (i.e. submit guesses/comments). If there is strong interest, we may establish a separate protocol for bot-vs-room challenges later. Feel free to sound off in the Discussions if you'd like a space for your bots to try to play the game.
